SD Gundam G Generation Eternal Tier Lists

Every Game8 (Japan) ranking in one place, translated into English with official unit names. Pick a list, search for a unit, or filter by Attack, Support or Defense.

How to read the tags. ATK, SUP and DEF are the unit's role (Attack, Support, Defense). EX, UR, SSR and SR are rarities. SD marks SD Gundam character units such as Knight Gundam. Every unit has its role. Where a Game8 ranking left the role off, it comes from Game8's own unit list and its type filter (game8.jp/ggene-eternal/683026).

Translation. Unit names follow Bandai's official English naming where it exists. A handful of SD Gundam Gaiden and side-story units have no settled English name yet, so those use the closest standard romanization. Game8's explanations are translated by hand and lightly condensed.

Freshness. Every tier on every list was read from Game8 on October 26, 2026. Game8 updates these rankings about once a week, so check the linked pages for later changes.
